Compute means the total number of floating-point operations (FLOPs) used in training.
Here, C = 6,685,202,495,569,920 FLOPs ≈ 6.685 × 1015 FLOPs is our baseline compute budget, counting forward and backward passes and excluding optimizer work. For example, ½C uses half this budget and 4C uses four times it. FLOPs count work; they are not FLOPs per second.

Method and limits

C = 6,685,202,495,569,920 measured forward/backward FLOPs. Compute budgets are 0.25C, 0.5C, C, 2C and 4C. Token budgets use each architecture’s measured FLOPs per token; rounding to full updates causes small differences from target compute.

Final validation uses a fixed 1,015,808-token sample. Lower loss is better. Winners and exploratory fits are recomputed from three-seed means where available and seed 42 elsewhere. This mixed replication and selection of the minimum can overstate precision; these are best tested configurations, not proven global optima.

Lines connect measured points, except the explicitly labeled power-law fits and 6N reference. They do not imply measurements between points. Point tooltips show exact values. Historical learning curves are for the original seed-42 winners, not averages of the repeated runs.
